Reduce visual intensity in a design that is too loud or overstimulating, without losing personality or turning generic.

## Visitor mode

- **Persuade + Experience:** more restrained palette, more whitespace, more typographic air. Drama is reduced, not removed; the POV stays.
- **Operate + Read:** less visual noise: fewer background accents, flatter cards, less colour, less motion. The tool disappears into the task.

## Assess

1. **Intensity sources:** saturation, contrast extremes, too many heavy elements, motion excess, decorative complexity, everything large with no hierarchy.
2. **Context:** purpose, audience (some contexts need energy), what already works, the core message to keep.

If any of this is unclear from the codebase, ask the user and wait.

Generic quiet looks like gray text on white, one sans at a uniform weight and a grid of identical cards. Restraint still needs a point of view.

## Plan

Decide which few elements stay bold and which recede, what to remove outright, and how restraint signals quality.

## Refine

**Colour**
- Shift saturation to 70–85%; replace bright colours with muted tones.
- Fewer colours; neutrals dominate, colour as accent (about 10%).
- High contrast only where it matters. Tinted grays instead of pure gray.
- Text on coloured surfaces follows [craft-floor.md](craft-floor.md).

**Weight**
- Lower font weights (900 → 600, 700 → 500) and sizes where appropriate.
- Hierarchy through weight, size and space rather than colour and boldness.
- More whitespace, lower density. Thinner, fainter or removed borders.

**Simplification**
- Remove gradients, shadows, patterns and textures with no purpose.
- Moderate radius extremes; flatten layering; drop glows, stacked shadows and blur.

**Motion**
- Shorter distances (10–20px instead of 40px); keep functional motion, drop flourishes.
- Easing and durations: [animate.md](animate.md). Stripping motion wholesale: `improve-animations` skill (Remove mode).

**Composition**
- Smaller scale jumps; bring rogue elements back to the grid; even out spacing rhythm.

**Avoid:** equal size and weight everywhere, grayscale, erasing personality, weakening affordances, making everything small and light.

## Verify

- Tasks are still easy.
- It still has character.
- Text is easier to read for long stretches.
- The POV survives the cuts.
