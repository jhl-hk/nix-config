> **Additional context needed**: the brand's emotional range.

Delight is product character revealed through a useful interaction, a humane response or a considered detail, at moments that earn it. Interaction craft lives in other skills: `micro-interaction` (feedback motion), `emil-design-eng` (small details), `improve-animations` Find mode (where to animate). Authored motion follows [animate.md](animate.md).

## Visitor mode

- **Persuade + Experience:** personality may run through voice, composition, motion and discovery while the artifact stays the focus.
- **Operate + Read:** concentrate delight at first use, completion, recovery or mastery.

## Find the opportunity

Inspect the target, DESIGN.md, product voice, repeat frequency and emotional context. Look for effort worth acknowledging, waiting that can inform, an error that needs empathy, an interaction whose response could express the brand, or a capability worth discovering. Do not celebrate an ordinary click.

## One delight thesis

State in one sentence what the user should feel and why that feeling belongs to this product. Deliver it with the smallest system: a distinctive response to a meaningful action, product-specific language, a recognizable material behavior, a detail grounded in the product world, or a discovery that reveals real utility. Derive it from product mechanism and visual world, not a stock catalog.

## By moment

- **Success:** match the response to effort and consequence. Routine saves just feel certain.
- **Waiting:** truthful progress or useful context. Never fake work or delay completion for a flourish.
- **Empty and first use:** see [clarify.md](clarify.md); the next action comes before personality.
- **Error and recovery:** problem and recovery first. No jokes about loss, money, privacy or blocked work.
- **Repeated interaction:** still satisfying on the hundredth use.
- **Discovery:** never hide required functionality.

## Limits

Delight must not delay or obscure the primary task, override platform conventions or accessibility, add unrequested factual claims, play sound without consent, become unskippable, or cost more in dependencies and assets than the moment is worth. Nonessential loops stop when hidden.

## Verify

- A neighboring product could not use the moment unchanged.
- It improves comprehension, confidence, motivation or recovery.
- The interface stays fast and obvious without it.
- Repetition does not turn charm into friction.
- Muted, keyboard, touch and localized paths work.
